XIN ZHUANG#

Doctoral researcher at the University of Helsinki, with over 10 years of experience in ecology and plant physiology. My PhD project integrates stable isotope tracing with advanced statistical modelling (signal filtering, LMMs, PCA, bayesianSEM, dynamic compartmental modelling, etc.) to track photoassimilate translocation under environmental stress and to unravel how water availability and carbon (C) source-sink dynamics mechanistically govern plant assimilation and resource allocation.
